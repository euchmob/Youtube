# Avatar: Seven Havens — series notes

Research notes for picking video ideas. Update as new episodes air (Book 1 drops: Oct 9 eps 1-3,
Oct 16 eps 4-6, Oct 23 eps 7-9, Oct 30 eps 10-13 — Paramount+).

## Premise
225 years after The Legend of Korra. A Cataclysm shattered the world; humanity survives in seven
fortress-cities ("havens"). The Avatar (Korra) is blamed for the Cataclysm and hated — people barely
know there were Avatars before her. Now the Avatar returns as **twins**: Pavi and Nisha, both
earthbenders by birth, both Avatars (first time ever two Avatars). Story starts in the haven **Ellora**.
Art style: brighter, gradient-heavy, Moebius-inspired. Arc symbol: diamonds (twins have diamond pupils).
Thematic motif: the seven chakras; Ellora = root chakra = **fear** (the Anomaly feeds on fear).

## Characters (personality → caption colour idea)
- **Pavi** — the Avatar we follow. Orphan, earthbender, hides her bending. Goofy, warm, hates fighting,
  has no interest in being the Avatar; afraid of the role ("I'm no warrior"). Wooden peg leg →
  metal prosthetic in ep 6. Green cloak, green eyes. Pet: Geet (cat-monkey-like). → warm gold/amber.
- **Nisha** — Pavi's twin. Secretly raised/trained as the Avatar by Karthik for 3 years. Serious,
  fierce, feisty earthbender, struggles with airbending. Turquoise Brigade uniform. Pet: Ruthi. → jade/turquoise.
- **Jae** — airbender in the Bending Brigade, raised to hate the Avatar, becomes the twins' ally.
  Workaholic, by-the-schedule; "processing all of this fantastically". Narrates the intro from ep 3. → sky/silver-white, soft blue glow.
- **Karthik** — Brigade commander (promoted ep 3), secret White Lotus member who raised Nisha.
  Ambiguous ("people think knowing the truth keeps them safe; usually the opposite"). → deep orange.
- **Brigade**: Uki (firebender, kidnapped by the Faceless in ep 4), Daemin (revealed White Lotus, ep 4),
  Lan. **Chancellor Kayoko** (Ellora's leader, red). **Dr. Jia** (ep 6). **The Faceless** (masked raiders).
- **The Anomaly** — red/black diamond energy entity; drives people mad with fear; twins are immune and
  see flashes of their lost past when near it. **Korra** appears in flashbacks (smirking in Avatar State).

## Episodes (Book 1)
1. **On the Horizon** — festival celebrating the *end* of the Avatar; Pavi hides; Jae sees her earthbend,
   chases her into the Harshlands; a tornado → Pavi airbends to save Jae → she's the Avatar.
   Twist ending: Nisha also bends all four elements → two Avatars.
2. **Into the Storm** — twins meet ("Why do you look like me?" … "sisters"), shared flashbacks, both have
   amnesia (~3 years). Pavi refuses: **"I can't be an Avatar. I'm no warrior. And the last one doomed the world."**
   The storm/Anomaly hits Ellora; inspired by ordinary people risking themselves to help, Pavi rejoins her sister.
3. *(title tbc)* — Karthik becomes Brigade commander; reveals to Jae he's White Lotus ("What do you know of
   the Order of the White Lotus?" / "a freaky underground cult that worships the Avatar"). Intro narration debuts:
   *"Most fear that they'll destroy the world again… but I believe they will save it."*
4. **The Faceless** — Faceless raid supplies, Brigade loses, Uki kidnapped; Daemin is White Lotus.
5. **The One Left Behind** — Jae & Karthik chase rumours; twins search Karthik's office for their records,
   find hidden room of Old World relics; message: "We have the Avatar… send this rattle raven back".
6. **Night of the Afflicted** — best-reviewed so far. Twins + Karthik trapped in Ellora's hospital with the
   Anomaly; Pavi learns she *lost* her leg ~3 years ago (not born without it); gets metal prosthetic.
7. **What Lies Beneath** — hidden Old World facility under Ellora; four-nation banners; footage of an older
   **Korra** as a symbol for the people — challenges the "Korra destroyed the world" story.
8-9. **The Envoys (Parts 1-2)** — diplomatic drama between havens, ancient ruins.

## Hottest theories (what fans argue about)
- **Korra didn't cause the Cataclysm** — scapegoat; or she was possessed / lost control of the Avatar State
  from too much spirit energy; her "psychotic smirk" is an unreliable narrator's version.
- **The Cataclysm split the Avatar spirit** → why there are two Avatars; Pavi & Nisha = two halves
  (yin/yang, Raava/Vaatu-like), one may lean "dark". Theory: Nisha is linked to the Anomaly.
- **The Anomaly = fear itself** (root chakra blockage) or connected to Vaatu.
- **What happened 3 years ago** — the twins' lost memories, Pavi's leg, their parents (killed protecting them?).
- **Karthik is not what he seems** (Red Lotus / dark side of the White Lotus); **Uki joined the Faceless willingly**;
  the Faceless have a mole; **Jae descends from Aang & Katara**.
- Old-World truth was purged on purpose; the Chancellor keeps the lie for power.
